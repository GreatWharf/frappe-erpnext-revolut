# Logo sources

- ERPNext: https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-16/erpnext/public/images/erpnext-logo.svg
- Revolut Business: https://developer.revolut.com/docs/resources/open-banking-logo-guidelines
- Revolut images are unmodified files from the official Business logo package.
- Logos identify the products this unofficial integration connects. They do not indicate endorsement and are not covered by this project’s MIT license.

- The integration header places the original ERPNext and Revolut Business vector logos on a shared canvas with a connector arrow.
- The app icon is the original Revolut Business app icon from the same official package.
